## Scope and method

All 24 rendered pages were reviewed in contact sheets. The 13 active figures and four main-text tables were checked in their final positions for legend placement, plot/axis overlap, panel labels, tick and label legibility, crowding, clipping, and caption spacing. Full-page checks included the revised run-in headings and the two separately restored full-width figures (Figures 12 and 13). Equations, section transitions, page breaks, conclusions, appendices, and the reference pages were also reviewed. The final source preserves the numerical content and scientific claims; this pass changes figure placement and run-in-heading punctuation only.

The manuscript retains the two-column visual hierarchy. Figures 12 and 13 remain separate, full-width floats at their original scale. Their sparse float-only pages are intentional: combining them caused Figure 13's caption to collide with the footer, while shrinking them would impair readability.

## Cross-page checks

- **Figures:** 13 active figures (Figures 1–13) checked in context. No legend covers a curve, marker, contour, axis, or annotation. Figure typography, ticks, panels, captions, and final-size readability were reviewed; no material clipping was observed.
- **Tables:** Four main-text tables checked for width, column alignment, readable labels, and clipping.
- **Structure:** Results proceed through the homogeneous baseline, anisotropy, aspect ratio/orientation, directional stop bands and design maps, heterogeneous Case C, steering, and micro-inertia. The Discussion explains the observed mechanisms and design relevance while retaining limitations.
- **Introduction and conclusions:** The table-free Introduction retains 50 unique cited works and the four ordinary contribution bullets. Conclusions remain a seven-item numbered list.
- **Limitations:** The unresolved energy-flux/group-velocity residual, unavailable raw harmonic fields, non-quantitative external comparisons, Case-C mesh sensitivity, and branch-specific scope of the micro-inertia speed limit remain explicit.
- **Page breaks:** Figures 12 and 13 use separate full-width float pages rather than a combined plate. No caption collides with a page number; no content was removed or reduced to force fit.
