# quasicrystals — discovery, structure, elasticity of QCs
- shechtman1984 — PRL 53:1951 (1984), 10.1103/PhysRevLett.53.1951: discovery of icosahedral phase.
- levine1984 — PRL 53:2477 (1984), 10.1103/PhysRevLett.53.2477: "quasicrystals" coined; new class of ordered structures.
- janot1994 — Oxford (1994): Quasicrystals: A Primer (2nd ed.).
- steurer2009 — Springer Ser. Mater. Sci. 126 (2009): Crystallography of Quasicrystals.
- deboissieu2012 — Chem. Soc. Rev. 41 (2012): phonons, phasons, atomic dynamics review.
- ding2000 — Rep. Prog. Phys. 2000: symmetry groups, property tensors, elasticity of QCs (incl. 2D-hex point groups).
