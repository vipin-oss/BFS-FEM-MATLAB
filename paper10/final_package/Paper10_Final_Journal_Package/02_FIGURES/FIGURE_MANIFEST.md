**Phase-A note (2026-09-26):** the original generation scripts mixed evanescent/complex-wavenumber modes into the dispersion diagrams (vertical line of points at kr/π ≈ 0) and the former branch tracker intermittently locked onto evanescent/higher-branch modes, producing ~10 spurious attenuation spikes in Fig. 4 at frequencies that are not band gaps. Both defects were fixed in the plotting/tracking layer only; the solver and all frozen datasets are byte-identical. Figs 3 and 4 were regenerated with the corrected classification and tracking (see `03_SOURCE_CODE/production/branch_utils.py`, `regenerate_phaseA_figures.py`); Figs 5, 7, 10 were regenerated with the same classification; Figs 6, 8, 9, 11, 13 are unaffected by the fix but were re-emitted by the corrected scripts with identical content.
